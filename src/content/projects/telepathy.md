Made for **PROCJAM 2025**.

## The idea

Everyone you pass on the street is thinking *something*. Telepathy lets you listen in. Wander the city, or choose a location and a time of day, then click on the people you meet. Their thoughts come back as full sentences, half-formed fragments, emotions, or images.

## How it works

The same toolkit as the [Victorian Nautical Archive](/work/victorian-nautical-archive/): seeded RNG, dice rolls, and Tracery-inspired text mashing, fed by large hand-written lists of interactions and thoughts. No generative AI.
